Flint is a city in Genesee County, Michigan, and is the county seat.

Flint is located along the Flint River, about 65 miles northwest of Detroit.

Flint was an important center of the American automobile industry, and General Motors was founded in Flint in 1908.

The University of Michigan-Flint is located in downtown Flint.
