1. Flint serves as the government center for Genesee County.
2. Flint has a population of approximately 79,735 residents.
3. Flint earned the famous nickname "Vehicle City" by becoming a world leader in manufacturing horse-drawn carriages and road carts in the late 1800s. The city later became the country's largest vehicle manufacturer.
4. Flint has a land area that covers 33.4 square miles.
5. The Flint Institute of Arts is the second-largest art museum in Michigan.
