The "Vehicle City" nickname originated in 1905, when Flint was the world's largest maker of horse-drawn carriages, producing over 100,000 a year at its peak.
[kupi.com]

General Motors was founded in Flint in 1908, and the city became a manufacturing powerhouse for GM's Buick and Chevrolet divisions. For a brief period, every Chevrolet and Buick was built in Flint.
[Wikipedia.org]

The Flint Sit-Down Strike of 1936–37 played a vital role in forming the United Auto Workers
[Wikipedia.org]

Flint's Lloyd Copeman invented the electric stove, the flexible ice cube tray, and the automatic toaster.
[Wikipedia.org]
