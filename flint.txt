General Motors was founded in Flint on September 16, 1908, by William "Billy" Durant.

Flint's automotive history developed from an earlier carriage industry that included the Durant-Dort Carriage Company.

The Flint sit-down strike of 1936-1937 was a pivotal event in American labor history and helped the United Automobile Workers gain recognition from General Motors.
