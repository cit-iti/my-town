Grand Rapids became the first city in the United States to add fluoride to its drinking water in 1945.
[https://www.grandrapidsmi.gov]


In 1881, the country's first hydro-electric plant came to life on the city's west side.
[https://www.grandrapidsmi.gov]


The city claims the first scheduled air service and the first publicly funded art installation.
[https://www.grandrapidsmi.gov]


Grand Rapids gained worldwide fame for fine furniture after an 1876 international exhibition in Philadelphia, and it remains a world leader in office furniture.
[https://www.grandrapidsmi.gov]
