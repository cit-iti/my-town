Staten Island is one of the five boroughs of New York City.

It is located in the southwest part of New York City and is separated from Manhattan by New York Harbor.

The Staten Island Ferry connects Staten Island with Lower Manhattan and is a major transportation route for residents and visitors.

Staten Island contains large park areas, including the Greenbelt, and is known as the least densely populated New York City borough.
