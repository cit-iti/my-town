Grand Blanc, Michigan

- A city in Genesee County, Michigan, just south of Flint. Population was
  8,091 at the 2020 census.
- Settled in 1822 and incorporated as a city in 1930.
- The name is French for "Great White." The area was originally home to
  Chippewa (Ojibwe) people; the name came from French traders.
- When Michigan became a state in 1837, Grand Blanc was considered for the
  state capital but lost to Lansing.
