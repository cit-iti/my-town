Linden was first settled in 1835. Brothers Richard and Perry Lamb were the first to settle the area, with Perry providing lodging for travelers.

Originally called "Warner Mills": The early community formed around 1840 when Consider Warner and Eben Harris platted the land and dammed the Shiawassee River to power the grist mill and saw mill. https://midmichigannow.com/news/local/historic-linden-pre-dates-the-civil-war

Cemetery created by accident: The Fairview Cemetery was established in 1836 after a worker named James Bell fell from a local mill into the Shiawassee River. There were no cemeteries established, so the family buried him on the spot.
