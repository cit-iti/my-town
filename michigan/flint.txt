Flint is the largest city and seat of Genesee County, Michigan, United States. Located along the Flint River, 66 miles northwest of Detroit, it is a principal city within the region known as Mid Michigan. 
- Flint was incorporated as a city in 1855.
- Flint became an important center of the automobile industry in the early 20th century.
- General Motors was founded in Flint in 1908.
- The 1936–1937 Flint Sit-Down Strike played an important role in the growth of the United Auto Workers.
