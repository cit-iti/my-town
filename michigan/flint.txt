Flint is the largest city and seat of Genesee County, Michigan, United States. Located along the Flint River, 66 miles northwest of Detroit, it is a principal city within the region known as Mid Michigan.

Flint began as a village established by fur trader Jacob Smith in 1819.
Lumber was a major industry in Flint during the nineteenth century.
Flint later became a manufacturing center for Buick and Chevrolet.
