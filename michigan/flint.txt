Flint, Michigan

-Flint was founded in 1819 when fur trader Jacob Smith set up a trading post on the Flint River.
-General Motors was founded in Flint in 1908 by William C. Durant.
-Flint is nicknamed "Vehicle City," and lighted arches spanning Saginaw Street downtown celebrate that name.
-The Flint Sit-Down Strike of 1936-37 led GM to recognize the United Auto Workers union.
-Kettering University, known for its engineering co-op programs, is located in Flint.
