Flint is the largest city and seat of Genesee County, Michigan, United States. Located along the Flint River, 66 miles northwest of Detroit, it is a principal city within the region known as Mid Michigan. Flint, Michigan, is the "Vehicle City" and the birthplace of General Motors, known for its significant role in the automotive industry. The city faced a major public health crisis between 2014 and 2016 due to lead contamination in its water supply. Still, extensive pipe replacement efforts have since ensured the water quality meets federal standards.
