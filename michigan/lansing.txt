Lansing, Michigan

-Lansing became Michigan's capital in 1847, when the legislature moved the capital from Detroit to a more central location.
-For its first year as capital, the town was officially named "Michigan, Michigan" before being renamed Lansing in 1848.
-The Michigan State Capitol building was completed in 1879 and is a National Historic Landmark.
-Ransom E. Olds founded the Olds Motor Vehicle Company in Lansing in 1897, the origin of Oldsmobile.
-The city sits where the Grand River and Red Cedar River meet, and parts of it extend into Eaton and Clinton counties.
