 1-	In 1925, the Detroit Edison Company built a hydroelectric dam on the Huron River to generate power. The resulting reservoir created Belleville Lake, a massive 1,200-acre waterway that is the largest inland lake in Wayne County.

2-	Every June, this small town of roughly 4,000 residents swells to welcome over 200,000 visitors for the National Strawberry Festival. The event features parades, carnivals, and endless strawberry-themed treats spread across the downtown peninsula and surrounding townships.
