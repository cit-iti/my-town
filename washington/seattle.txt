Seattle, Washington

- Seattle was originally called New York-Alki before the settlement was moved and renamed Seattle.
- The Great Seattle Fire of 1889 destroyed buildings across 116 acres of the city's business district, but no one died in the fire.
- Seattle has seven vehicular movable bridges that can open for marine traffic.
- The Great Blue Heron became Seattle's official city bird in 2003 after winning a public contest against the common crow.
- About 70% of the drinking water supplied to the Seattle area comes from the Cedar River Watershed.
