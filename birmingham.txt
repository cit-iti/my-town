"In 1871 the city was founded, and by the turn of the 20th century, Birmingham grew so fast that residents claimed the town sprung up by magic!"

" The city sits in Jones Valley, famously known for being one of the only places in the world to find all the ingredients to make iron – coal, iron ore, and limestone."

"The oldest baseball field, Rickwood Stadium, is in Birmingham! Home to the Birmingham Black Barons, the team was among the most successful in the Negro Southern League. "
