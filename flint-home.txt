Flint is located in Genesee County, Michigan, and traces its beginning to a village founded by fur trader Jacob Smith in 1819.

The city developed from a lumbering center into a major producer of carriages and automobiles, which helped give Flint the nickname "Vehicle City."

The University of Michigan-Flint was founded in 1956 and grew into one of the University of Michigan's three campuses.
